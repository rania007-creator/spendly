Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

When you use Spendly you provide personal information such as your name, email address, and any expense details you enter. All this data is stored securely in a SQLite database on the server and is accessible only to you. No personal data is sent to third parties without your explicit consent.

How We Use Your Data

The data you enter is used solely to provide the expense‑tracking functionality of the app: categorizing transactions, generating reports, and storing historical records for your personal use.

Data Storage

All user data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server. We enforce a retention policy where deleted entries are removed permanently. Users can export or delete their data via the account settings (future feature).

Third‑Party Services

Spendly does not rely on any external APIs or services in its current configuration. If new third‑party integrations are added in the future, they will be documented here.

Contact Us

For questions about privacy or data handling, reach out to support@example.com.
